International Patients

If you are travelling to Istanbul — or already living here — and want your allergy or asthma complaints properly evaluated by an adult allergy specialist, this page explains how a visit works: what to do before you travel, what happens during a single visit in Nişantaşı, and how follow-up is handled after you return home.

Who is this service for?

Three typical situations

Visitors combining travel with care

You are visiting Istanbul for business or holiday and want to use the trip for a proper allergy work-up — testing, diagnosis and a written treatment plan, ideally within a single visit.

Expats living in Istanbul

You live and work in Istanbul, your complaints (blocked nose, cough, hives, food reactions) have been going on for months, and you would like to discuss them in English.

Patients seeking a second opinion

You already have test results or a diagnosis from your home country and want an independent evaluation, or you are considering allergen immunotherapy and want to discuss whether it is appropriate for you.

Please note: the clinic sees adult patients only. For patients under 18, a paediatric allergy and immunology specialist should be consulted.

Plan your visit

Three steps, from your home to ours and back

Most of the value of your trip is decided before you get on the plane — a short exchange in advance lets us plan testing for your very first visit.

  1. 1

    Online pre-assessment — before you travel

    Contact us by WhatsApp or phone and share your complaints, your medication list and any existing test results. We review them, tell you honestly whether a visit is worthwhile, and advise which medicines (such as antihistamines) should be paused before skin testing.

  2. 2

    Single-visit diagnostics — in Istanbul

    History and examination, then skin prick testing where indicated (about 30 minutes, results read the same day) and, if needed, a lung function test. You leave with a diagnosis discussed face to face and a written treatment plan. Where appropriate, allergen immunotherapy can be initiated.

  3. 3

    Follow-up guidance — after you return home

    You receive a treatment summary you can share with your own physician. Laboratory results that arrive after your departure are communicated to you, and practical questions about your plan can be clarified remotely. Ongoing treatment at home is coordinated with a local physician.

About the online pre-assessment: it is a planning step, not a medical examination — no diagnosis is made and no treatment is prescribed remotely. It does not replace a face-to-face consultation, and it is not suitable for emergencies. If you have sudden shortness of breath, swelling of the tongue or throat, or feel faint, call your local emergency number immediately (in Türkiye: 112).

Preparation

What to bring with you

The more of your history we can see, the fewer tests need repeating. None of these items is obligatory — but each one makes your visit more efficient.

Checklist

  • Previous test results — allergy tests, blood work, lung function tests, imaging reports.
  • A complete medication list — including over-the-counter medicines, sprays and supplements; photos of the boxes are perfectly fine.
  • Translated reports, if available — English translations are ideal; if you only have originals in another language, bring them anyway and we will work through them together.
  • A short symptom diary — when the complaints started, in which season, place or situation they get worse. Even a few notes on your phone help.
  • Your questions — write them down in advance so nothing is forgotten during the consultation.

In an emergency

Sudden shortness of breath, swelling of the tongue or throat, difficulty speaking or swallowing, widespread rash with faintness — these may be signs of anaphylaxis. Do not wait and do not message us: call 112 (the emergency number in Türkiye) immediately.

Frequently asked questions

Before you book your flight

Do I need a referral to book an appointment?

No. This is a private outpatient clinic; you can contact us directly by phone or WhatsApp to request an appointment. Bringing your previous medical records is helpful but not required for booking.

Is same-day allergy testing possible?

Often yes — provided the schedule allows and antihistamine medicines have been stopped for about 10 days beforehand. This is exactly why we ask you to contact us before you travel: with a short online pre-assessment we can plan testing for your first visit instead of losing days in Istanbul.

How long does testing take?

The skin prick test takes about 20–30 minutes and the result is read after roughly 15 minutes, within the same visit. A lung function test, if needed, adds another 10–15 minutes. Blood tests (specific IgE) take a few working days depending on the laboratory; if the result arrives after your departure, it is communicated to you and discussed remotely.

Should I stop antihistamines before skin testing?

Yes — antihistamines should generally be stopped about 10 days before a skin prick test, otherwise the result may be falsely negative. But please ask us first: do not stop blood pressure, heart or asthma medicines on your own. Tell us every medicine you take during the pre-assessment and we will advise you individually before your trip.

Can immunotherapy be continued in my home country?

To be honest with you: allergen immunotherapy runs for 3–5 years, so continuing it abroad requires coordination with a physician in your home country who agrees to administer and monitor the treatment. Where appropriate, treatment can be initiated in Istanbul and you receive a detailed summary for your local physician. Whether this is realistic depends on your diagnosis and on the availability of a suitable physician at home — we assess this openly with you before any decision is made, not after.

Do you see children as well?

No. The clinic sees adult patients only. For patients under 18, we recommend consulting a paediatric allergy and immunology specialist.
