Services

What is treated at the practice

The practice sees adult patients only (18 years and over). Patients under 18 are kindly referred to a paediatric allergy specialist.

Diagnosis

Allergy skin testing

Skin prick testing identifies the allergens behind your symptoms — pollens, house dust mites, moulds, pet dander and more. The test takes 20–30 minutes, does not draw blood, and results are read in about 15 minutes, during the same visit. Blood testing (specific IgE) is available when skin testing is not suitable.

Treatment

Immunotherapy (allergy shots)

Allergen immunotherapy retrains the immune system against the allergen responsible for your symptoms, rather than only suppressing them. It is a long-term treatment, typically lasting 3–5 years. Suitability is decided only after examination and testing; response varies from person to person.

Ongoing care

Asthma care

Diagnosis and long-term management of adult asthma: pulmonary function testing, inhaler technique review, written treatment plans and regular follow-up. Allergic rhinitis and asthma are assessed together, as upper and lower airways behave as one system.

Diagnosis & treatment

Urticaria, food & drug allergy

Evaluation of chronic hives (urticaria), eczema, and suspected reactions to foods or medications. The goal is to name the actual trigger — or to rule allergy out — so that you are not left avoiding things unnecessarily.

How it works

A 3-step care path for international patients

Designed so that as much as possible is prepared before you travel, and as much as possible is handled remotely after you return home.

  1. 1

    Online pre-assessment

    Before you travel, share your complaints and any previous test results. This helps plan your visit — for example, which medications to pause before skin testing — so your appointment is not wasted.

  2. 2

    Single-visit testing & treatment start

    In Istanbul: consultation, allergy skin testing (results in about 15 minutes) and, where appropriate, pulmonary function testing. You leave with a written diagnosis and treatment plan; treatment can begin the same day when suitable.

  3. 3

    Remote follow-up guidance

    After you return home, written guidance on how to continue your plan is provided, including coordination notes you can share with your local physician for ongoing care.

Please note: Online pre-assessment does not replace physical examination or definitive diagnosis; it cannot be used for emergencies.
